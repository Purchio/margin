# Margin overview videos

- [20-second overview](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/449f58af-3ed7-4229-9d72-a28fc728949c): an animated preview of the library, reading and Pencil markup.
- [30-second launch video](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fb0d9cf1-9a46-439d-bb96-029b6f6e3afd): the landscape launch edit, combining real iPad screen and camera footage with animation, music and sound effects.

The 20-second overview and preview stills are illustrative motion-design assets, not recordings of a running app. The interface and sample book are recreated in HTML, and the audio was synthesized by local Python scripts.

The 30-second launch video includes footage of Margin running on an iPad, with Apple Pencil writing and highlighting, plus animated sections. Books and handwritten marks visible in the footage are part of that recorded demo. No App Store availability is claimed.
